To the American public, the Foreign Affairs Manual (FAM) - the operating manual for the State Department - has always been edited silently. State of the art for granular comparison has been something along the lines of a manual side-by-side of two 10,000 page documents that are each split across 1,000 webpages. In other words, granular comparison was not feasible. This opacity prevents accountability, as not knowing what changes have been made to State Department policy (by current admin) makes those changes harder to oppose. 

This tool makes Foreign Affairs Manual changes transparent. It shows exactly what changed and at what times, so decisions that affect American foreign policy can't hide behind de facto secrecy anymore. Input two dates and the Internet Archive compiles the full FAM for each and redlines it for you, as well as showing you the most substantive changes, how many changes were made per subsection, and other relevant analytics.

https://github.com/shinyben/whats-up-fam

The push of the changes from Biden to Trump admin seem to be a rollback in protections for trans people as a protected class.
