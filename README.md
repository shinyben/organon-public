# Organon

This repo builds my website, [organon-public](https://shinyben.github.io/organon-public/)

Organon in Ancient Greek means instrument, tool, organ, or “systematic framework for acquiring knowledge.” It is the title of Aristotle’s six works on logic (Categories, On Interpretation, etc.) and is in the opening line to a Kate Bush song. I’ve had mine since 2017, and am planning on using “organon-public” to surface certain parts of it. I have a little over 7,000 notes in my private organon.
